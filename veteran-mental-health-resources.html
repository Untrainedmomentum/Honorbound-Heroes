Crisis & Immediate Help

2 programs — start here if you need help now

Veterans Crisis Line

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs · 24/7

Call 988 press 1, text 838255, or chat online. Free, confidential support for veterans and their families in crisis — staffed by trained responders, many of them veterans.

VeteransFamiliesFree24/7
veteranscrisisline.net ↗

SAMHSA National Helpline

Substance Abuse & Mental Health Services Administration

Free, confidential treatment referrals for mental health and substance use disorders. Connects veterans to local resources. Available in English and Spanish, 24/7.

VeteransFamiliesFree24/7
samhsa.gov/find-help ↗

VA Mental Health Programs

2 programs

VA Mental Health Services

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs

Same-day access for urgent needs. Covers PTSD, depression, anxiety, substance use, and more. Walk in to any VA Medical Center day or night — no appointment needed for urgent care.

VeteransFree
va.gov/mental-health ↗

VA Vet Centers

VA Readjustment Counseling Service · 300+ locations

Community-based counseling centers offering free individual and group therapy. No VA enrollment required, no discharge status requirement. Also serves families.

VeteransFamiliesFree
Find a Vet Center ↗

Free Nonprofit Counseling

3 programs — no VA enrollment required

Give an Hour

National nonprofit · Licensed volunteer therapists

Connects veterans and military families with licensed mental health professionals who donate their services. No insurance, no VA enrollment, no discharge requirement. In-person or telehealth.

VeteransFamiliesFreeTelehealth
giveanhour.org ↗

Headstrong Project

National nonprofit · PTSD focused

Up to 30 free therapy sessions for post-9/11 veterans seeking PTSD treatment. Works with over 2,500 clients annually. In-person or telehealth depending on your state.

VeteransFreeTelehealth
getheadstrong.org ↗

Cohen Veterans Network

National network of mental health clinics

Network of nonprofit mental health clinics treating PTSD, depression, anxiety, and relationship issues. Serves post-9/11 veterans and their families. Sliding scale fees — many receive care free.

VeteransFamiliesTelehealth
cohenveteransnetwork.org ↗

Telehealth & Digital Tools

2 programs — access from anywhere

Roger — Stop Soldier Suicide

Nonprofit telehealth · Free counseling

Free telehealth mental health counseling for veterans and active-duty service members, provided by the nonprofit Stop Soldier Suicide. No insurance required.

VeteransFreeTelehealth

PTSD Coach App

VA National Center for PTSD · Free mobile app

Free VA-developed mobile app providing PTSD education, self-assessments, coping tools, and crisis resources. Available 24/7, anonymous, no login required.

VeteransFree24/7App
ptsd.va.gov/ptsdcoach ↗
