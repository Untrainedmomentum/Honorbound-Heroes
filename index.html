The Crisis

These Americans fought for us. We have to fight for them.

Veterans represent roughly 8% of the adult homeless population, yet they are among the most underserved. Many came home changed — by trauma, injury, and a system that wasn't ready for them. HonorBound Heroes exists to close that gap in our own backyard and beyond.

32,882

veterans counted homeless on a single night in January 2024 — a 7.5% drop from 2023 (HUD PIT Count)

13,851

Our Mission

Compassion that goes beyond a cardboard sign

At HonorBound Heroes, we believe that dignity is not a privilege — it's something every veteran earned the moment they raised their right hand. Our mission is to honor their service by meeting them where they are, with real help and a real path forward.

We don't just write checks. We show up. From emergency care kits to job placement support to long-term housing advocacy, we walk beside veterans at every step of their journey home.

Our Approach

Three tiers of impact, one unbreakable commitment

We believe in winning battles you can actually fight — starting with immediate relief, building toward lasting change. Every step forward matters.

Our First Campaign

Care Kit Drive

Raising funds to assemble and distribute emergency care kits to homeless veterans in our community. Simple, direct, immediate.

  • Hygiene essentials, warm clothing, and food
  • Every dollar raised goes to kits
  • Distributed directly to veterans in need
Growing With Purpose

Connecting Veterans to Resources

Meeting veterans where they are and pointing them toward the help that exists — benefits, services, and community support they may not know how to reach.

  • VA benefits awareness
  • Local resource referrals
  • Community outreach events
What We're Working Toward

A Home for Every Veteran

Our mission doesn't end at a care kit. We're committed to being part of the larger fight to end veteran homelessness — one step, one person, one community at a time.

  • Housing advocacy
  • Transitional support partnerships
  • Long-term community presence
Our Story

Started with heart. Rebuilding with purpose.

HonorBound Heroes began the way most great ideas do — with a group of people who looked at a problem in their community and refused to look away. They saw veterans sleeping in parking lots, under bridges, invisible in the richest country in the world, and they said: not here, not on our watch.

One man standing is still a movement waiting to happen.

The organization hit hard times. Founders moved on. Momentum stalled. But the mission never changed, and neither did John McAllister — the one constant through it all. John has carried HonorBound Heroes on his back through the difficult stretch, refusing to let it become just another good idea that faded away.

Now we're rebuilding — with new leadership, a clearer strategy, and a community ready to step up. The best chapter of this organization is the one we haven't written yet. And we'd like you to help write it.

John McAllister

Executive Director

John has kept the HonorBound flame alive through difficult times. His commitment to veterans' dignity and his refusal to quit are the foundation this organization is rebuilding on.
