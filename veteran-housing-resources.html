Veteran Housing & Homelessness Resources

Every program below is free and available nationally. Click any card for what to have ready and how to navigate the process — not just a link, a real guide.

Veterans Crisis Line

988 → Press 1

Free · Confidential · 24/7 · Housing specialists available

Emergency & Transitional Housing

3 programs

HUD-VASH

HUD + Department of Veterans Affairs

Section 8 housing vouchers for homeless veterans, paired with VA case management. The largest federal veteran housing program in the US.

VeteransFree
va.gov/homeless/hud-vash ↗

Grant & Per Diem (GPD)

VA Homeless Programs Office

VA-funded local nonprofits providing transitional housing and services for homeless veterans. Use the VA locator to find a provider near you.

VeteransFree
va.gov/homeless/gpd ↗

Volunteers of America

National nonprofit · 300+ locations

Transitional and permanent supportive housing for veterans nationwide. One of the country's largest veteran housing providers.

VeteransFamiliesFree
voa.org/veterans ↗

Permanent & Supported Housing

3 programs

SSVF — Supportive Services for Veteran Families

VA · Nationwide grantee network

Rapid rehousing and homelessness prevention for very low-income veteran families. Covers rental assistance, deposits, utilities, and case management.

VeteransFamiliesFree
va.gov/homeless/ssvf ↗

NCHV — National Coalition for Homeless Veterans

National advocacy + provider network

Connects veterans to a nationwide network of community-based housing providers. Includes a state-by-state resource locator.

VeteransFamilies
nchv.org ↗

Soldier On

National nonprofit · Peer-support model

Permanent supportive housing communities for homeless veterans using a peer-support model — veterans helping veterans stabilize and rebuild.

VeteransFree
wesoldieron.org ↗

Financial Assistance & Legal Aid

2 programs

Veterans Benefits Administration

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs

Disability compensation and pension that can stabilize housing. Many homeless veterans are eligible for benefits they have never claimed.

VeteransFree
va.gov/benefits ↗

Legal Services Corporation

Federal nonprofit · Veteran access program

Free civil legal aid: eviction defense, benefits claims, discharge upgrades. Discharge status affects eligibility for nearly every VA housing program.

VeteransFamiliesFree
lsc.gov/veteran-resources ↗

For Family Members & Caregivers

2 programs

PCAFC — Caregiver Assistance

VA Caregiver Support Program

Monthly stipend, health insurance, and respite care for caregivers of eligible post-9/11 veterans. Can stabilize households at risk of losing housing.

Family caregiversFree
caregiver.va.gov ↗

211 Helpline

United Way · Nationwide

Dial 211 from any phone to reach local housing, food, and utility assistance. Works for families even if the veteran can't make the call.

FamiliesFree24/7
211.org ↗
