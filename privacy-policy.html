Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Effective immediately upon posting

Who We Are

HonorBound Heroes is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN: 99-1356980) headquartered in Remus, Michigan (Mecosta County). Our website is located at honorboundheroes.com. We can be reached at contact@honorboundheroes.org.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, donate through our donation platform, volunteer, or contact us.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number when you submit a volunteer or board interest form
  • Name, email, and donation amount when you donate through our third-party donation platform (GiveButter)
  • Any information you choose to include in form messages or correspondence with us

We do not directly collect or store payment card information. All financial transactions are processed by GiveButter, which operates under its own privacy policy and security standards.

We may also automatically collect non-personal technical information such as browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages through standard web analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries and follow up on volunteer or board applications
  • To send donation receipts and acknowledgment letters required by the IRS
  • To send organizational updates, newsletters, or mission-related communications (you may opt out at any time)
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • To comply with legal obligations, including IRS reporting requirements for 501(c)(3) organizations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not use your information for commercial advertising purposes.

California Residents (CalOPPA & CCPA)

Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • Right to Know: You may request information about the personal data we have collected about you in the past 12 months
  • Right to Delete: You may request deletion of personal data we have collected, subject to certain exceptions
  • Right to Opt Out: We do not sell personal information. No opt-out is required for data sales
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@honorboundheroes.org. We will respond within 45 days.

European Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your personal data. Our legal basis for processing personal data is legitimate interest (responding to inquiries) and consent (newsletter communications).

To exercise your GDPR rights, contact us at contact@honorboundheroes.org. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

While HonorBound Heroes is a US-based nonprofit, if any EU resident donates to or interacts with our website, GDPR obligations apply. We take this seriously and handle all data accordingly.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to understand site usage. By using our website, you consent to the use of these technologies. You may configure your browser to refuse cookies, though some site functionality may be affected.

We do not use advertising cookies or allow third-party advertisers to track visitors on our site. Claude.ai products used in development are ad-free.

Third-Party Services

  • GiveButter — our donation platform. Their privacy policy governs data collected during donation transactions
  • Formspree — our form submission service. Data submitted via our forms is processed by Formspree under their privacy policy
  • Mailchimp — our email platform (being set up). Email subscribers are subject to Mailchimp's privacy policy
  • Microsoft 365 — organizational communications are processed through Microsoft's infrastructure

Data Retention

We retain donor records and correspondence for a minimum of 7 years as required by IRS regulations for nonprofit organizations. Volunteer inquiry data is retained for up to 3 years. You may request deletion of your data outside of legally required retention periods.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
