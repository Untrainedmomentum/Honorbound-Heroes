About HonorBound Heroes

HonorBound Heroes is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 99-1356980) headquartered in Remus, Michigan (Mecosta County). Our mission is ending veteran homelessness through emergency relief, resource connections, and housing advocacy.

Donation Terms & Tax Deductibility

Donations to HonorBound Heroes are generally tax-deductible under IRC Section 501(c)(3). Consult your tax advisor regarding deductibility. All donations are voluntary and non-refundable absent extraordinary circumstances determined solely by HonorBound Heroes.

Donor Acknowledgment: For donations of $250 or more, HonorBound Heroes will provide a written IRS-required acknowledgment letter. Ensure your contact information is accurate when donating.

Quid Pro Quo Disclosure

A quid pro quo contribution occurs when a donor receives goods or services in exchange for a donation. If HonorBound Heroes receives a quid pro quo contribution exceeding $75, we will provide a written disclosure stating the deductible amount and a good faith estimate of the fair market value of goods/services received, as required by IRC Section 6115.

Currently, all donations through our website are purely charitable — no goods or services are provided in exchange. The full donation amount is potentially tax-deductible.
