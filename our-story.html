Our Story

One man standing is still a movement waiting to happen.

HonorBound Heroes began the way most great ideas do — with a group of people who looked at a problem in their community and refused to look away. They saw veterans sleeping in parking lots, under bridges, invisible in the richest country in the world, and they said: not here, not on our watch.

The organization hit hard times. Founders moved on. Momentum stalled. Funds ran low. For a while, it looked like HonorBound Heroes might quietly become another good idea that faded away before it had a chance to matter.

"The mission never changed. And neither did I."

John McAllister stayed. Through the departures, through the doubt, through the stretch where there was no revenue and no roadmap — John kept the organization alive because he believed what it stood for was too important to let die. He has carried HonorBound Heroes through its most difficult chapter on nothing but commitment.

Now we're rebuilding — with new leadership coming in, a clearer strategy, and a community that's starting to show up. We've launched our website, established our digital presence, and are assembling a board of directors. The first care kit drives are being planned. The re-engagement of past donors has begun.

The best chapter of this organization hasn't been written yet. And we'd like you to help write it.

Our Approach

Three tiers of impact, one unbreakable commitment.

We believe in winning the battles you can actually fight — starting with immediate relief, building toward lasting change. Every step forward counts.

Right Now

Care Kit Drive

Raising funds to assemble and distribute emergency care kits to homeless veterans in our community. Simple, direct, immediate.

  • Hygiene essentials, warm clothing, and food
  • Every dollar raised goes to kits
  • Distributed directly to veterans in need
Growing With Purpose

Connecting Veterans to Resources

Meeting veterans where they are and pointing them toward the help that exists — benefits, services, and community support they may not know how to reach.

  • VA benefits awareness and navigation
  • Job connections and referrals
  • Community outreach events
The North Star

A Home for Every Veteran

Our mission doesn't end at a care kit. We're committed to being part of the larger fight to end veteran homelessness permanently.

  • Housing advocacy and partnerships
  • Transitional support programs
  • Long-term community presence
